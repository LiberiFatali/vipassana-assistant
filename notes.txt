A few open threads for when you're ready to build the chatbot itself:

- Course schedule is dynamic — the site links to schedule.vridhamma.org for actual dates, so the chatbot may need a live fetch tool rather than static data
- Two languages — the site serves Vietnamese and English users; the chatbot may need bilingual capability
- Registration flow — the site is vague ("visit /courses and follow instructions"); you may want to clarify what the actual registration steps look like
- Scope boundary — should the chatbot handle general Vipassana questions (e.g., about Goenka globally) or strictly UCENLIST-specific?
